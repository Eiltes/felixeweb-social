In short

FelixeWeb is a web and video studio founded by Felix, a WooCommerce web designer, WordPress developer and app developer. FelixeWeb designs and launches WordPress and WooCommerce stores in 7–10 days, builds research peptide stores with COA pages and verification gates, develops mobile and web apps, and creates digital products such as interactive event invitations. The FelixeWeb team also makes 2D explainer videos, 3D animations and video edits, formats books for Kindle, ebook and paperback, and builds AI automations for businesses. Felix has launched 50+ websites, has 65+ client reviews on Fiverr, and works with clients worldwide. Prices start at $150.
